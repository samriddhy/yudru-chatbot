YuDru Technologies: Elevating Indigenous Drone Innovation

Founded in April 2025 and headquartered in New Delhi, *YuDru Technologies* is a fast-rising innovator in the aerospace and defense sector. The company is dedicated to building a completely self-reliant, end-to-end drone ecosystem that bridges the gap between cutting-edge research and real-world deployment.

Driven by a mission of localization and technological sovereignty, YuDru specializes in hardware prototyping, AI-driven navigation, and advanced flight control systems. By combining high-performance aerial platforms—spanning custom FPVs, surveillance systems, and heavy-duty logistics drones—with certified professional pilot training, YuDru Technologies is not just manufacturing UAVs; they are shaping the future of unmanned aviation from the ground up.

PRODUCTS AND PLATFORMS:
- Custom FPV drones for racing and surveillance
- Heavy-duty logistics drones for cargo delivery
- Surveillance UAV systems for defense applications

CORE CAPABILITIES:
- Hardware prototyping from scratch
- AI-driven navigation systems
- Advanced flight control systems
- Professional UAV pilot training and certification
